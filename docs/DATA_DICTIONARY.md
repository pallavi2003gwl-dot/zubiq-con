## Why two levels

The six headings on ZubiQ's website are a **category** layer, not a service catalogue.
At category level the client base looks well served: the average active client holds
**2.9 of 6 categories**. Drop one level and the same client base holds **4.8 of 30
sub-services**, roughly 16% penetration.

That difference is the commercial argument for the whole tool. "This client has GST" is
true and useless. "This client files monthly GST returns, crossed ₹2 crore turnover, and
has never bought the mandatory GST annual return and reconciliation" is an action.

It is also the reason the prototype is not a spreadsheet. Six categories against fifty
clients is a pivot table. Thirty sub-services against fifty clients, each with its own
statutory trigger and turnover condition, is not something anyone maintains by hand.

## `sub_services.csv`

The 30-row catalogue. Five sub-services per category, drawn from the service lists
published on ZubiQ's website.

| Column | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| sub_service_id | SS0101–SS0605. First two digits after SS are the category. |
| service_id | → service_categories |
| service_category | Denormalised join key |
| sub_service_name | |
| base_annual_fee_inr | Indicative fee, sizes the upsell opportunity |
| is_recurring | Yes/No |
| is_entry_service | Whether clients typically arrive through this |
| **is_statutory** | Yes where the service is legally mandatory once a condition is met. **This is the most important column in the dataset.** A statutory gap is not a sales pitch, it is a compliance warning, and that makes it the most credible thing the AI can raise with a client. |
| **relevance_trigger** | The plain-language condition that makes the service apply (for example "GST turnover above Rs 2 crore"). Fed directly into the AI data context so recommendations cite the actual reason. |

The 30 sub-services:

| Category | Sub-services |
|----------|-------------|
| Taxation | ITR Filing, Advance Tax Planning, TDS/TCS Compliance, Notices & Assessments, NRI Taxation & Capital Gains |
| GST | Registration, Monthly/Quarterly Returns, Annual Return & Reconciliation, E-invoicing & E-way Bill Setup, ITC Review & Litigation |
| Bookkeeping | Monthly Books Closure, Financial Statement Preparation, Payroll Processing, AP/AR, Bank Reconciliation |
| Incorporation | Pvt Ltd/OPC Registration, LLP Registration, ROC Annual Filings, DIN/DSC & Statutory Registers, Startup India Recognition |
| Audit | Statutory Audit, Tax Audit u/s 44AB, Internal Audit, Stock & Inventory Audit, Due Diligence |
| Virtual CFO | Budgeting & Forecasting, Business Valuation, Fund-raise Support, Investor & MIS Reporting, Working Capital Planning |

## `leads.csv` — built backwards from the chatbot

Every column is tagged below as **CHATBOT** (a website bot can ask it in conversation),
**DERIVED** (computed on arrival), or **INTERNAL** (added by the firm afterwards).
`docs/CHATBOT_SPEC.md` turns the CHATBOT rows into an actual conversation flow.

| Column | Origin | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| lead_id | DERIVED | |
| enquiry_date | DERIVED | Timestamp of the session |
| contact_name | CHATBOT | |
| phone | CHATBOT | **Required.** The single field without which a lead is worthless. |
| email | CHATBOT | |
| company_name | CHATBOT | `-` for individual enquiries |
| entity_type | CHATBOT | Also routes the rest of the conversation |
| industry | CHATBOT | |
| city | CHATBOT | |
| annual_turnover_band | CHATBOT | Asked as a band, never as an exact figure. People will not type their turnover into a chatbot but will pick a range. Drives value estimation and audit-applicability. |
| service_category | CHATBOT | First-level menu choice |
| sub_service_interest | CHATBOT | Second-level choice. This is what makes AI personalisation specific instead of generic. |
| sub_service_id | DERIVED | |
| urgency | CHATBOT | Immediate (deadline within 15 days) / This month / This quarter / Just exploring. **The highest-signal question in the flow** and the one most often left out of lead forms. |
| existing_ca_status | CHATBOT | No CA / Have one, considering a switch / Have one, want a second opinion / Prefer not to say. A switching lead and a first-time lead need completely different handling. |
| preferred_contact_mode | CHATBOT | WhatsApp / Phone / Email |
| preferred_contact_time | CHATBOT | Morning / Afternoon / Evening. Trivial to ask, materially improves connect rates. |
| notes | CHATBOT | Free-text description of the problem. This is the raw material the AI personalises on. Without it every recommendation would be a template. |
| consent_to_contact | CHATBOT | Yes/No. Needed before anyone calls or messages. |
| source | DERIVED | Where the visitor came from: Website Direct, Google Search, Instagram, LinkedIn, Referral, Walk-in. Captured from the referrer, not asked. |
| capture_method | DERIVED | Website Chatbot / Website Contact Form / Phone Call / In Person |
| chatbot_completion | DERIVED | Complete / Partial / NA. Whether the visitor finished the flow. |
| status | INTERNAL | New, Contacted, Qualified, Converted, Lost |
| last_contact_date | INTERNAL | Blank for New leads. Drives staleness and the recency score. |
| assigned_to | INTERNAL | Blank for New leads |
| estimated_value_inr | DERIVED | Sub-service base fee scaled by turnover band. Not guessed. |

### Deliberate incompleteness

Partial chatbot sessions have blank `annual_turnover_band`, `urgency`,
`existing_ca_status`, `preferred_contact_mode`, `preferred_contact_time`, and `notes`.
New leads have blank `last_contact_date` and `assigned_to`.

This is intentional and the build must handle it. A real chatbot loses people mid-flow,
and a dashboard that breaks on a blank cell is a dashboard that breaks on day one.
It also produces a genuine finding, see below.

## The findings planted in the data

Use these to verify the build is computing correctly.

### Category-level alignment (Module 4 headline)

| Category | Revenue | Rev share | Reach share | Gap | Posts | Eng rate | Leads | Conv |
|----------|---------|-----------|-------------|-----|-------|----------|-------|------|
| Audit | ₹20.2L | 28.9% | 1.0% | **−27.9** | 3 | 2.0% | 7 | 43% |
| GST | ₹19.4L | 27.8% | 4.1% | **−23.7** | 6 | 4.0% | 17 | 24% |
| Bookkeeping | ₹12.9L | 18.5% | 5.3% | −13.2 | 10 | 3.2% | 10 | 20% |
| Taxation | ₹9.4L | 13.4% | 30.2% | +16.7 | 24 | 3.7% | 16 | 12% |
| Virtual CFO | ₹4.0L | 5.7% | 13.9% | +8.3 | 14 | **8.9%** | 6 | **50%** |
| Incorporation | ₹4.0L | 5.7% | **45.5%** | **+39.9** | 30 | 2.6% | 24 | 21% |

Four findings fall out of this:

1. **Incorporation is over-invested.** Nearly half of all social reach goes to the
   category producing 5.7% of revenue. It is also one-time work, so the effort buys no
   recurring income. This is the headline.
2. **Audit and GST are under-served.** Together they are 57% of revenue and 5% of
   attention. The two services that pay the firm's bills are close to invisible.
3. **Virtual CFO is untapped demand.** Best engagement rate in the dataset at 8.9%,
   more than 3x Incorporation, and the best conversion rate at 50%, but only 6 leads in
   six months. The audience responds and is never asked to act.
4. **Taxation is seasonal volume, not value.** Second-highest reach, lowest conversion
   at 12%. A lot of attention from people who file once and leave.

### Sub-service penetration (Module 2 headline)

Average active client: **2.9 of 6 categories**, but **4.8 of 30 sub-services**.

Six statutory rules alone surface **70 opportunities worth ₹16.79L** in annual fees
across 46 active clients. Specific gaps planted:

| Gap | Why it is credible |
|-----|-------------------|
| Files ITR, no Advance Tax Planning | Mandatory once liability crosses ₹10,000. Clients pay 234B/234C interest without it. |
| Files GST returns, turnover above ₹2Cr, no Annual Return & Reconciliation | Statutory. A live compliance exposure. |
| Incorporated through ZubiQ, no ROC Annual Filings | Mandatory every year for every company and LLP. The most defensible upsell in the dataset: the firm registered the company and then stopped. |
| Private Limited, no Statutory Audit | Mandatory for every Pvt Ltd regardless of turnover. |
| Turnover above ₹1Cr, no Tax Audit u/s 44AB | Statutory threshold. |
| Books maintained, no Financial Statement Preparation | Mandatory annually for companies and LLPs. |
| Virtual CFO held shallow | Whoever has it usually holds one sub-service of five. Fund-raise Support, Business Valuation, and Investor & MIS Reporting have near-zero penetration despite the strongest social signal. |

### Chatbot completion finding (a bonus, and a real one)

| Completion | Leads | Converted |
|------------|-------|-----------|
| Complete | 30 | 7 (23%) |
| Partial | 12 | 0 (0%) |
| NA (non-chatbot) | 38 | 12 (32%) |

Leads who abandon the chatbot mid-flow convert at zero. Twelve is a small sample and the
report should say so rather than overclaim, but the direction is clear and the
recommendation writes itself: capture phone number early in the flow, before the
qualifying questions, not after. That is a concrete chatbot design decision that came
out of the data, which is exactly what a reverse build is supposed to produce.
